# Frequently Asked Questions #

## Can declarations be made consecutively?

Yes, with limits. A win ends the round, so nothing can follow it.
Chow, pung and claimed kong can only be followed by a concealed kong or an added kong; a concealed kong or an added kong can be followed by a concealed kong, an added kong, or a win.

## Can Robbing a Kong rob a concealed kong?

No; no winning shape may rob a concealed kong.

## After a chow, may one discard a two-gapper tile?

Yes; there is no restriction on the tile discarded after a declaration.

## Does Nine Gates count only the nine-sided waiting shape?

Yes; only the nine-sided waiting shape is Nine Gates.

## Does Thirteen Orphans count only the thirteen-sided waiting shape?

No; the idea of Thirteen Orphans is the ultimate low efficiency from winning, so you only need to assemble it.
